Boost Your Immunity Naturally

Keeping your immune system strong is essential for overall health. Here are simple ways to naturally boost your immunity:

  • Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains.
  • Get regular physical activity (at least 30 minutes a day).
  • Sleep 7–8 hours every night for proper recovery.
  • Stay hydrated and reduce processed sugar intake.
  • Practice stress management techniques such as meditation or yoga.

Remember: consistency is the key to a stronger immune system.
